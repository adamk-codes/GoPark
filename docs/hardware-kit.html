GoPark Hardware Kit

What to buy for the detection demo, what to avoid, and which jobs need a person's hands. Tick items as you go; ticks are saved on this phone only.

How detection works: two sensors at the gate catch every car going in or out, and a camera reads the plate to say whose car it is. BLE beacons are no longer needed.

Shopping list

Do not buy

  • AirTag, Tile or Galaxy SmartTag. They use closed networks, so our app can't detect them.
  • MIFARE Classic 1K NFC tags. iPhones can't read them. Get NTAG213/215.
  • Charge-only USB cables. They power the ESP32 but can't upload code to it.
  • Cheap non-official Pi power supplies. They cause random crashes from low voltage.

Jobs only a person can do

Claude can write all the code and flash boards plugged into this laptop. It can't touch the phones, the parking lot, or anything that needs a password or a signature.

What Claude handles

  • All the code: beacon, NFC and geofence handling in the app; ESP32 beacon and gate firmware; the rotating gate QR page; the Pi plate pipeline; the trip simulator.
  • Exact settings to type in: beacon UUID, Major and Minor numbers, power levels, NFC URLs, wiring pins.
  • Flashing the ESP32 once it's plugged into the laptop running Claude Code.
  • Setting up the Raspberry Pi over SSH once it's on Wi-Fi and added as a connection.
  • Step-by-step test scripts for the field test, and turning the logs into accuracy numbers and charts.
  • Debugging from error messages, logs and screenshots you send.
